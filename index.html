Project overview

About the data

This project explores the global landscape of fuel prices and taxation from 2020 to 2026. Compare petrol, diesel and liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) costs across countries and regions, and investigate how taxation, subsidies and income levels relate to fuel affordability.

📌

Key insights

Identify regional differences in fuel prices and tax burdens, and examine how policy choices can influence what consumers pay at the pump.

Methodology & interpretation

A quick guide to understanding what the dashboard can and cannot tell you.

What is included?

The project description states that the dataset contains 27,468 complete records across 84 countries and 7 regions for 2020–2026. Variables cover fuel prices, tax percentages, regional differences, income levels and subsidy structures.

  • Petrol, diesel and LPG price comparisons
  • Changes over time and between regions
  • Taxation and affordability indicators

How should findings be used?

Use the visualizations to identify patterns and questions for further research. Correlation between taxes and prices does not, by itself, prove that one causes the other. Consider currency, units, reporting dates, subsidies and local market conditions when comparing countries.

  • Check selected years and filters before comparing values
  • Verify definitions and source data for policy decisions
  • Treat forecasts as estimates, not guarantees
